LidGuard RGB baseline model - research demonstration.

The model was fitted to the training partition of the 100-image RT-BENE
evaluation subset documented in results/rgb_data_manifest.json.

RT-BENE dataset: Kevin Cortacero, Tobias Fischer, Yiannis Demiris (2019).
Source: https://zenodo.org/records/3685316
Paper: RT-BENE: A Dataset and Baselines for Real-Time Blink Estimation in
Natural Environments, ICCV Workshops 2019.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCVW.2019.00147

The classifier uses RGB eye crops. It has not been validated on NIR images,
clinical corneal exposure, or ICU patients. PopEYE is not used by this model.
