What this prototype demonstrates

  • Eye-state estimates from manually selected RGB eye crops.
  • Continuous open-state timing and an acknowledgment workflow.
  • A visible “unable to assess” state for rejected or stale input.
  • Local processing and event export without saved camera frames.

Corneal visibility, partial opening, protection from dressings, and performance in ICU patients require separate validation.
