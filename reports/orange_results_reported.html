Conclusion

All four models performed similarly (accuracy 85.9% to 88.7%, AUC 0.928 to 0.974), showing that image-embedding features separate open from closed eyes well. Logistic Regression led in accuracy and MCC, while SVM led in AUC.

Note: results come from cross validation on 71 images, a small sample, so they should be confirmed on a larger test set.
